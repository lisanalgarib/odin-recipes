Guacamole

An easy guacamole recipe that saves me lots of time. This dish satisfies everyone in the family. Try it tonight!

Making perfect homemade guacamole doesn’t have to be tedious. This top-rated easy guacamole recipe comes together quickly with a relatively short ingredient list.

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe avocados
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1-2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1-2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Cut the avocados in half, remove the pit, and scoop the flesh into a bowl.
  2. Mash the avocado with a fork until smooth.
  3. Add the chopped onion, minced garlic, diced tomatoes, and lime juice to the bowl.
  4. Mix all ingredients together until well combined.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  6. Serve immediately or cover and refrigerate for later use.
  7. Enjoy your delicious homemade guacamole!
