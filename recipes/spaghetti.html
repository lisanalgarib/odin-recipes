Spaghetti

An easy spaghetti recipe that saves me lots of time. This dish satisfies everyone in the family. Try it tonight!

Making perfect homemade spaghetti doesn’t have to be tedious. This top-rated easy spaghetti recipe comes together quickly with a relatively short ingredient list.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 10 spaghetti noodles
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the spaghetti noodles according to package instructions.
  3. In a skillet, brown the ground beef over medium heat. Drain excess fat
  4. Layer the ingredients in a baking dish.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven for 30 minutes.
  6. Remove from oven and let it cool for 10 minutes before serving.
  7. Garnish with fresh basil and serve warm.
  8. Enjoy your delicious homemade spaghetti!
